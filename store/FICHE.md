## Justification des capacités restreintes (demandée à la soumission)
- **runFullTrust :** Apex Radio est une application de bureau (Win32) : elle lit la mémoire partagée des jeux de course (Le Mans Ultimate, Assetto Corsa EVO, Automobilista 2) et l'interface locale de Le Mans Ultimate (localhost) pour donner les annonces de l'ingénieur de course.
- **unvirtualizedResources :** l'application enregistre les setups de voiture qu'elle crée directement dans le dossier du jeu Le Mans Ultimate, et ses données dans Documents\Apex Radio, pour que l'utilisateur et le jeu les retrouvent. Sans cela, le jeu ne verrait pas les setups.

## ENGLISH

**Name:** Apex Radio

**Short description:**
Your voice race engineer for Le Mans Ultimate, Assetto Corsa EVO and Automobilista 2. Free, hands-free, on your radio.

**Description:**
Apex Radio puts a real race engineer in your headset. While you drive, it talks to you like an endurance team on the radio: strategy, fuel and virtual energy, tyres, gaps, flags, spotter… and you can ask it questions with your voice.

• Live pit strategy: when to box, how much fuel or energy, how many stops.
• Pit menu set automatically in Le Mans Ultimate ("prepare the stop", "keep the tyres", "repair everything").
• Spotter: car left, car right, three wide.
• Faster cars called early in multi-class racing ("Hypercar coming, 5 seconds").
• Gaps with driver names, positions, pace, lap times, personal bests.
• Sector yellow flags, safety car, blue flags.
• Driving tips in engineer language: "you're braking too early into turn 5", "use the apex kerb".
• Car balance analysis and setup suggestions.
• Stint planner for team endurance races.
• Voice questions: "how many laps of fuel?", "gap to P3?", "tyre condition?".
• Speech recognition runs on your PC: your voice is never sent anywhere.
• Phone page, OBS overlay and Stream Deck support.

Supported games: Le Mans Ultimate, Assetto Corsa EVO, Automobilista 2.
Languages: French, English.

**Keywords (7 max):** race engineer, simracing, Le Mans Ultimate, spotter, Assetto Corsa EVO, Automobilista 2, voice
